# MIDI Universal System Exclusive - defined by the MIDI 1.0 spec, vendor independent.
# These are safe to send to anything; the Inquiry makes a device identify itself.
@device Universal SysEx (MIDI spec)
@channel 1
@delay 30

Device Inquiry        : F0 7E 7F 06 01 F7
Master Volume         : F0 7E 7F 04 01 00 VV F7 : 0 127 127
Master Balance        : F0 7E 7F 04 02 00 VV F7 : 0 127 64
GM System On          : F0 7E 7F 09 01 F7
GM System Off         : F0 7E 7F 09 02 F7
GM2 System On         : F0 7E 7F 09 03 F7
